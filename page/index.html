Abstract

Recent state-of-the-art few-step video generators are primarily built upon consistency distillation (e.g., rCM for bidirectional models and self-forcing for causal models). Although effective, consistency distillation exhibits limited scalability with respect to inference-time budgets and often introduces detail artifacts in generated videos.

To address these limitations, we propose AnyFlow, the first any-step video post-training framework. AnyFlow complements base Flow Map training (e.g., MeanFlow) with on-policy distillation, ensuring broad data distribution coverage while simultaneously enhancing visual quality. Unlike the consistency backward simulation typically used in previous on-policy distillation methods, we exploit the merits of two-time variables to support any-step backward simulation. This approach significantly improves both quality and scalability with test-time compute.

We validated AnyFlow across a diverse range of video diffusion models—spanning bidirectional and causal architectures from 1.3B to 14B parameters, as well as text-to-video and image-to-video tasks. AnyFlow produces a series of models that surpass both their consistency-based counterparts and the original base models in quality. These results demonstrate the significant potential of Flow Map distillation for video generation.
